Controller files and matching button anchors are unchanged copies of the Confidential Mission VR4ALL bundled assets.

The Silent Scope launch instruction pictures in picture/silent-scope-how-to.png
are rendered adaptations of these controller models, shown beside the user's
rifle models. Yellow interaction highlights and left-handed mirroring are added.
